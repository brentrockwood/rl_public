Who

Rockwood Lab is led by Brent Rockwood, a software engineer and technology leader with more than two decades of experience building production systems for large enterprises, government, and commercial software vendors.

Most recently, Brent served as a VP, Lead Information Security Engineer at Wells Fargo, where he helped design and build internal platforms for cybersecurity metrics, data visualization, and deployment workflows across large Hadoop-backed systems. His work there supported both operational teams and executive decision-makers.

Prior to that, he delivered full-stack Node/TypeScript projects for Fortune 50 retail and banking clients through Teksystems, led backend efforts on automotive and airline projects at Soltech, and spent years developing commercial identity management and governance products at Quest/Dell Software.

Alongside enterprise work, Brent has maintained a long-running freelance practice building election reporting tools, identity connectors, and other specialized applications. Rockwood Lab is the continuation of that work: a small, focused practice that combines software craftsmanship with practical prototyping.
